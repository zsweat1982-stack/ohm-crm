# VNPHC: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: PROVEN = our own account data. LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (a proxy, not profit). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect reach signal. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us in this style, not a competitor's ad.

Hard rules that override every format (plan guardrails and brain): zero client imagery, no anonymized client stories, staff footage only with a signed release, never imply Medicare pays for private duty home care, no fear words (no shock, secret, deadly, neglect bait), no invented prices, years, awards or hours. Wisdom of the Elders: no current clients on camera, signed releases from every guest. Alzheimer's content gives no medical advice and implies no cure.

## Graphic and static formats

Our own data (PROVEN, small sample, FB Jul 1 to Sep 30, Sprout Nov 8): the top posts were question-led and family-led ("Most seniors want to age at home" 374 views; a dementia diagnosis families post 312; "Memories may fade. The need to feel safe..." 283). Questions families ask beat promotional posts. Reach is small, so treat it as direction only.

Use these, varied week to week. Large high-contrast type (the audience is adult daughters 40 to 65 on phones).
1. **Family question card.** EXAMPLE: "What do you ask a home care agency before you sign?" then "Who covers if the caregiver can't come? Who answers the phone? What is the minimum?" then "Call 404-658-5800 or visit visitingnursephc.com."
2. **Who pays, in plain words.** EXAMPLE carousel, 4 cards: "Who pays for home care?" / "Private duty home care is private pay." / "VA benefits may help for eligible veterans (VERIFY wording with Katie)." / "Not sure what applies? Call 404-658-5800." Never say or imply Medicare covers it.
3. **Checklist or send-this card.** EXAMPLE: "5 questions families forget to ask a home care agency." PROXY: resource-list format drew 207K plays and 7,321 saves on a small account.
4. **Caregiver month card for the one who answers the phone.** EXAMPLE: "For the one who answers the phone. Short, regular breaks help. Ask us about respite care." (Family Caregivers Month.)
5. **Respite care explained.** EXAMPLE: "Respite care: a few hours so you can rest." Keep it practical, no promise of outcomes.
6. **Staff or Wisdom of the Elders quote card.** A pull quote from a released guest, text over a plain warm background. No clients.
Photos: staff only with release, office and community. Never a client, never a stock photo of "a patient".

## Reels: the hook system

- Two hooks at once. **Audio hook** = the first spoken line from the talking head (staff, owner or a released Wisdom of the Elders guest). **Visual hook** = different text on screen in the first second, so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print the words being spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "Your mom's caregiver calls out at 6 a.m. Here is who you reach at VNPHC." Visual: "WHO COVERS?" Audio: "Three things to ask any home care agency before you sign." Visual: "ASK THESE 3". Audio (Ana, Alzheimer's talking head, release first): "Here is how we start when a family tells us a parent is forgetting more." Visual: "WHERE TO START".
- No talking head: first frame is an object or room (a window, a clock, lamps going on, a phone screen), never a person who is a client. Text card is the only hook.
- 8 words or fewer per card, a new card or shot every 2 to 3 seconds. First frame is the hook. No logo intro.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify before final): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge. Hook upper-middle, centered. Burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = the hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram. Facebook hashtags follow the weekly pack, up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio": "Call 404-658-5800 or visit visitingnursephc.com."
- First line carries the main keyword (private duty home care Atlanta, respite care Atlanta, Alzheimer's care Atlanta).
- Holidays (Veterans Day, Thanksgiving) are thank-you only, no service pitch.
