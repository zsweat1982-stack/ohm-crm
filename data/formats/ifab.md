# iFab: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: PROVEN = our own account data (small samples). LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (none found). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us in this style, not a competitor's.

Plan look: the build is the hero. Scale, texture, process. Let the work fill the frame. Rules: only projects in the archive or on ifabatl.com; no client names, logos or branded product without written OK; no timeline or price claims unless the owner confirms; crew faces only with their OK.

## Graphic and static formats

Our own data (PROVEN, small samples, FB Oct 15 to Nov 6): a person on camera answering a question and drawing-to-build process won ("Real rock or shotcrete?" 1,327; "make the client happy" 1,305; "every build starts with a drawing and a pen" 862; drawing to weld 615). Direction: show who builds it and how.
1. **Question card.** EXAMPLE: "Real rock or shotcrete?" then "Both look like stone. Only one is built to last outdoors." (VERIFY the claim with the owner; safer: "Can you tell? Swipe for the answer.")
2. **Render vs built carousel (5 cards).** EXAMPLE: "The render" / "The drawing" / "The build" / "The install" / "The finished room." Only from the archive.
3. **Scale shot with a person.** EXAMPLE: "How big is it? Stand next to it." Crew only with OK.
4. **Process card.** EXAMPLE: "Every build starts with a drawing and a pen." Photo of the drawing beside the weld or form. Our own best caption angle.
5. **What we build.** EXAMPLE: "Scenic fabrication. Decorative concrete. Exhibitry. Metalwork. Call 478-787-8909 or visit ifabatl.com." (Services are from the site.)
6. **Itemized-scope card.** EXAMPLE: "What is in the number? Scope, materials, install, site cleanup." Only if the owner confirms the practice.
Real shop photos only. Branded-client work (Coca-Cola Music Hall bottle, hardware logo, Moet booth, Porsche pencil) needs written clearance first.

## Reels: the hook system

- Audio hook = the first spoken line from the talking head (Chief Creative or founder). Visual hook = different text on screen in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is being said.
- Example pair for the "Real rock or shotcrete?" format. Audio: "People ask me if this rock is real." Visual text: "REAL ROCK?" Example pair for process. Audio: "Every build starts with a drawing and a pen." Visual: "FROM A SKETCH".
- No talking head: first frame is the work (spray POV, a weld, a stain pass, scale shot), never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = hook frame. Reels are archive re-cuts (nothing new is filmed in November), stills fallback.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio": "Call 478-787-8909 or visit ifabatl.com." (VERIFY which phone.)
- First line carries the keyword (scenic fabrication Atlanta, themed environments, custom exhibit fabrication).
- Holidays are thank-you only.
