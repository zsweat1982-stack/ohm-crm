# JL Adams: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (none found). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us, not a competitor's. There is no own-account data, so nothing is PROVEN.

Rules: no invented prices, years or awards; the "thirty-five years" and "over 30 years" claims conflict, so use none until the owner confirms. Testimonials need a signed release. Real jobs only.

## Graphic and static formats
1. **Septic question card.** EXAMPLE: "When did you last pump your septic tank?" / "Hartwell and Hart County. Call 706-245-0109." 
2. **One call, four trades.** EXAMPLE: "Plumbing. Electric. Excavation. Septic. One local company." (the four site services)
3. **Land clearing before and after.** EXAMPLE: "Cleared and graded." Same angle, real job.
4. **What is in the price.** EXAMPLE: "Septic pumping: what the price covers." Only with the owner's real numbers.
5. **Crew card.** EXAMPLE: "Meet the plumbers." (release).
6. **Local callout.** EXAMPLE: "Hartwell. Hart County. Lake Hartwell."

## Reels: the hook system
- Audio hook = first spoken line from the talking head (owner or crew lead). Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "This is what a full septic tank looks like." Visual: "WHEN DID YOU LAST PUMP?" Audio: "We cleared this lot in a day." Visual: "BEFORE AND AFTER" (only if true).
- No talking head: first frame is the truck, the tank lid or the cleared lot, never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third. Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)
- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio": "Call 706-245-0109 or visit jladamsinc.com."
- First line carries the keyword (plumber Hartwell GA, septic pumping Hart County).
