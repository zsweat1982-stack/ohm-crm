# Nitro Pro Racing: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (none found). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us in this style, not a competitor's. There is no own-account data for Nitro Pro, so nothing is PROVEN.

Plan look: fast and loud. Race action and tech tips for people who know the hobby. Hard rules: check every number against Nitro Pro published data (commenters catch errors); LiPo safety needs client sign-off, never call a battery "safe"; the weekly best-seller product video is new footage and must be real footage, no AI-redrawn parts, light grade; a Black Friday offer only if the client confirms in writing; no warranty claims until read and confirmed; Jared Tebo's name only with his OK; no competitor names.

## Graphic and static formats

1. **Spec card with units (EPIC1 only, site-confirmed).** EXAMPLE: "EPIC1" / "787 oz torque at 8.4V" / "0.079 sec per 60 degrees at 8.4V" / "Brushless. Billet aluminum. IP67." (all four from the site, VERIFY before use). CTA: "nitroproracing.com."
2. **Bench card.** EXAMPLE: "On the bench." A photo of the servo on a test stand. Real photo only.
3. **Which servo for which class.** EXAMPLE carousel (5 cards or fewer): "1/10 offroad" / "1/10 onroad" / "1/8 offroad" / "1/8 onroad" / "Not sure? Ask us." Model names and numbers only from published data.
4. **Racer proof card.** EXAMPLE: "Built by racers. For racers." (site wording, VERIFY) over a race photo from the team page.
5. **Offer card.** Only if confirmed: "Free shipping on servo and battery orders over $249. Code FREESHIP." (site wording, confirm it is live) and the terms line.
6. **Thank-you card (Veterans Day, Thanksgiving).** No sale.
OBSERVED competitor patterns: percent-off Black Friday graphics (Direct RC, up to 20% through Dec 22) and product-on-black spec cards (AMain/ProTek). Nitro Pro's price is 2x to 15x budget servos, so spec proof, not discount, is the contrast.

## Reels: the hook system

- Audio hook = the first spoken line from the talking head (racer, bench tech or client rep with OK). Visual hook = different text on screen in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "This is what 787 ounces looks like on the bench." Visual: "REAL NUMBERS" (only with the EPIC1 numbers confirmed). Audio: "Here is why a servo strips its gears." Visual: "GEAR TRAIN 101" (needs client confirmation). Audio: "Pick your servo by class." Visual: "1/8 OR 1/10?"
- No talking head: first frame is the car on the track, the servo on the bench or a close-up of the spec, never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds. Race audio on; no music swell.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website, never "link in bio": "Shop at nitroproracing.com." No phone is on the home page (VERIFY a number). Include the spec with its unit and voltage whenever a number appears.
- First line carries the keyword (RC servo, 1/8 buggy servo, brushless servo).
- Offer captions end with the terms line. Holidays: thank-you only.
