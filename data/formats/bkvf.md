## Graphic and static formats

Our own data (PROVEN, small samples, IG Jul 1 to Sep 30): collaborator-tagged Instagram videos and cause-first story posts carry reach: "If we don't shine a light on it, it never changes" (12,697), the gala "Tonight's the night" (5,818), "NO ONE CARRIES IT ALONE" (3,842).
1. **Program explainer card.** EXAMPLE: "Connection is Prevention" / "A weekly, clinically led, peer-supported group for Veterans. Free and confidential." / "Learn more at bkvf.org." (Wording from the site and the CIP page edit note; VERIFY with the client.)
2. **Quiet quote card.** EXAMPLE: "No one walks alone." Plain text on a calm background. Crisis block in the caption.
3. **Veterans Day thank-you.** EXAMPLE: "To every Veteran: thank you." No ask, no sale, no donate link. Photos from the Aiken Veterans Day Parade (8 photos from Rob Vaughan in Basecamp, Nov 8) once cleared and checked for Jenn.
4. **Where it goes, with proof.** EXAMPLE: "Where your gift goes" with figures from BKVF's own report. Only after the client supplies the source (Form 990 or audited statement). Meta only.
5. **Event recap.** EXAMPLE: "Classic Golf Tournament, Oct 26." Real photos, people with OK, no Jenn.
6. **Peer voice quote card.** A line from a signed-release sit-in interview (TN sit-downs, Luke Henderson talking head).
OBSERVED competitor pattern we do not copy: statistic-led headlines ("an estimated 32,000 veterans could be on the streets") and a named-person story about a client. BKVF's safe-messaging rules ban suicide statistics, and no one can be retold without a release.

## Reels: the hook system

- Audio hook = the first spoken line from a released talking head (Luke Henderson, TN sit-down interviews). Visual hook = different text on screen in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is being said. Alternate interviews across weeks; do not stack two back to back.
- Examples. Audio: "I was the last one to ask for help." Visual: "ASKING FIRST" (only with the speaker's release and the crisis block in the caption). Audio (golf tournament): "Here is what 100 people on a golf course did for Veterans." Visual: "WHY WE PLAYED". Audio (CIP): "Here is what happens at a Connection is Prevention meeting." Visual: "NO ONE WALKS ALONE".
- No talking head: first frame is the quiet shot (hands, a flag, a table, a parade banner), never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, a new card or shot every 2 to 3 seconds. Music low, no swell.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = the hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5 per the weekly pack.
- CTA is the website, never "link in bio": "Learn more at bkvf.org." No phone number is on the site's home page text (VERIFY a number before adding one). Donation CTA on Meta only: "Give at bkvf.org."
- First line carries the keyword (veteran mental health, Connection is Prevention).
- Any post about help, suicide or PTSD ends with the crisis block verbatim. Holidays: thank-you only.
