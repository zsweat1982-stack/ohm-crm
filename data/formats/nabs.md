## Graphic and static formats
1. **Doula explainer card.** EXAMPLE: "What does a birth doula do?" / "Support before, during and after birth." / "North Atlanta Birth Services."
2. **Questions to ask a doula (carousel, 5 cards or fewer).** EXAMPLE: "Who will be at my birth?" / "What is the backup plan?" / "What do you cover before labor?" / "What is in the price?" / "Can my partner meet you first?"
3. **Childbirth education card.** EXAMPLE: "Childbirth education in North Atlanta." Dates only if real.
4. **Birth story quote card.** A first-person line from a released client. Name optional.
5. **Postpartum week one card.** EXAMPLE: "Week one at home."
6. **Local callout.** EXAMPLE: "North Atlanta families, we are local."

## Reels: the hook system
- Audio hook = first spoken line from the talking head (Hannah or a doula). Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "If you are scared of giving birth, here is what a doula does." Visual: "WHAT A DOULA DOES". Audio: "Three questions to ask before you hire a doula." Visual: "ASK THESE 3".
- No talking head: first frame is a soft detail (a hand on a shoulder is a person: only with release), a birth bag, a clock or a text card, never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third. Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)
- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio" (website and phone not recorded; VERIFY).
- First line carries the keyword (birth doula North Atlanta, doula Atlanta).
