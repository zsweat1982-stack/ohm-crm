# Sun and Shield: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: PROVEN = our own account data. LONG-RUNNER = competitor ads that kept running 365+ days (a proxy, not profit). RULE = OHM standard or platform guidance. EXAMPLE = copy written by us in this style, not a competitor's ad.

## Graphic and static ad formats

What our own account says (PROVEN): the top organic Instagram graphic was "Still driving without protection?" (33,197 views), a question headline. Organic wins with a clear close-up of the car. Source: Sprout, pulled Nov 8.

What the long-running competitor ads lead with (LONG-RUNNER, 74 ads, moderate to low confidence): no clear hook 19, authority 13, price 8, question 8, offer 7, problem 6, showcase 5, local callout 3. Quote-style calls to action lead (Get quote 27). Price plus offer together showed up in 6 of the 26 longest runners.

Use these six, and vary them week to week:

1. **Question headline** (design: editorial or notes_app). EXAMPLE: "Still driving without protection?" then under it "XPEL paint protection film, ceramic coating and window tint in Alpharetta." Button line: "Get a quote: (678) 392-4700".
2. **Offer graphic** (design: ticket_offer or big_number). Only when an offer is confirmed. EXAMPLE: "$100 OFF CERAMIC COATING" / "with any full-vehicle window tint package" / "Code THANKS" / terms line. Competitor pattern: Perfect Touch Window Tint ran "$100 Off Ceramic IR Tint" for 808 days (LONG-RUNNER).
3. **Local callout** (design: color_poster). EXAMPLE: "ALPHARETTA TESLA AND RIVIAN OWNERS" / "XPEL certified for both." Competitor pattern: "Queens Car Owners! Save $200" ran 470 days. Our credential line is on the site: XPEL Certified Tesla and Rivian Network Partner.
4. **Authority proof** (design: review_card or grid_card). EXAMPLE: "XPEL Georgia Dealer of the Year, 2024" with a real install photo. Authority was the second most common long-runner hook.
5. **Before and after or finished car** (design: polaroid_wall or annotated). Real photos only. EXAMPLE headline: "Same hood. Same day. Water beads like this." Showcase works with a close-up (PROVEN pattern).
6. **Explainer carousel** (5 cards or fewer). EXAMPLE: Card 1 "Which tint film fits your car?" / Card 2 "Dyed: budget, blocks glare" / Card 3 "Carbon: matte finish, no metal" / Card 4 "Ceramic: clearest view, blocks more heat" / Card 5 "Not sure? Call (678) 392-4700".

Do not copy: race-to-the-bottom prices ($99 to $249 full-car tint appears in long-runners). Sun and Shield is a premium XPEL shop.

## Reels: the hook system

- Every Reel has two hooks at once. The **audio hook** is the first spoken line from the talking head. The **visual hook** is text on screen in the first second, worded differently, so a sound-off viewer still gets a reason to stay. Do not print the same words that are spoken.
- No talking head? The text on screen is the only hook, and the first frame must show the car or the result, not a logo.
- First frame is the hook. No logo intro, no music swell. One idea per Reel.
- Text cards: 8 words or fewer, one idea each, a new card or shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta's Reels safe zones, verify before final): keep text out of the top 14% and bottom 35% of the frame, and away from the right edge where buttons sit. Put the hook in the upper-middle, centered. Captions burned in sit in the middle third, never over the bottom caption area.
- Cover frame is the hook frame. Text, highlight and cover follow that week's color.
- Example pair for a "Georgia tint law" talking head. Audio hook: "Most people in Georgia get this tint law wrong." Visual hook text: "THE 32% RULE". 

## Captions (RULE, from the Sun and Shield brain)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram unless told otherwise; Facebook and the 5-hashtag rule apply per the weekly pack.
- Call to action is the website plus the phone number, never "link in bio." Quote-style wording wins: "Get a quote at sunandshieldautomotiveprotection.com or call (678) 392-4700."
- First line carries the main search keyword (for example "window tint Alpharetta" or "XPEL paint protection film").
- Every offer caption ends with its terms line. Holidays are thank you only, no offers.
