# CISGA: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days. OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us in this style, not a competitor's. There is no own-account data for CISGA, so nothing is PROVEN.

Plan look: warm and hopeful. Adults on camera only. Alumni voices lead. Hard rules: NO student face, voice, name, work, silhouette, back of head or hands (photos, video, audio, drawings, student work). Alumni on camera only with a signed release and CISGA approval; do not name their school or district without both. Donation asks on Meta, never TikTok (TikTok is awareness only). Every number needs a source on screen. Do not borrow the national network's ratings or "99%" figures. No promos or offers without client OK. Approvals in Basecamp (Adrienne and Jamie).

## Graphic and static formats

1. **Number-with-source card.** EXAMPLE: "42 school districts. 38 Georgia counties." / "Source: cisga.org (VERIFY current figures with the client)." No photos of children.
2. **Alumni quote card.** EXAMPLE: a short quote in an alumnus's words, text over a plain warm background, with their release on file. Name and school only if both approve.
3. **Adult supporter or partner card.** EXAMPLE: "A company, a school wish list, one fall supply drive." Adults only. (Atlanta employers ask for a school partner; CISGA has a School Wish Lists page.)
4. **What your gift did.** EXAMPLE: "What your gift did this year" with a CISGA-supplied number and source. Meta only for the ask.
5. **Thank-you card.** EXAMPLE: "Thank you to our donors, volunteers and site coordinators." No ask. (Donors leave when they never see what the gift did.)
6. **Awareness days.** EXAMPLE (World Kindness Day, Nov 13): "Kindness shows up on a school day." Text only. Reshare supporter posts on awareness days (starter rule).
OBSERVED competitor pattern we do not copy: photo cards of children and "a child in our program is waiting for you."

## Reels: the hook system

- Audio hook = the first spoken line from an adult talking head (alumnus with release, staff, board member, volunteer). Visual hook = different text on screen in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print the spoken words.
- Examples. Audio: "Someone noticed I was missing from class, and that changed things." Visual: "WHO NOTICED?" (alumnus only, released). Audio: "Here is what a site coordinator does in a school." Visual: "INSIDE THE SCHOOL" (adult staff, no students in frame). Audio (volunteer): "I mentor for a few hours a month." Visual: "A STEADY ADULT".
- No talking head: first frame is text, a map of Georgia, a number card or an empty school hallway with no students; never a logo or a child. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new card every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = hook frame. TikTok carries awareness only: no donate button, no "give" CTA.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website, never "link in bio": "Learn more at cisga.org." No phone number was recorded (VERIFY). Donation CTA on Meta and email only: "Give at cisga.org."
- First line carries the keyword (dropout prevention Georgia, volunteer mentor Georgia).
- Holidays and awareness days: thank-you or awareness only unless the client approves an ask.
