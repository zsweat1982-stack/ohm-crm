## Graphic and static formats

Our own data (PROVEN, small sample, FB Sep 15 to Oct 7, under 500 views each): the top posts were a foundation and kitchen quality post (480), "the hardest decision before anyone pours a footing" (454), "treetops to trusses" (447), and Chris on camera "Integrity isn't a tagline" (355). Direction: process and decision content from a builder who shows the work.
1. **Question card.** EXAMPLE: "What does 'included' mean in a custom home quote?" under it: "Ask for allowances in writing before you sign." CTA: website plus phone (VERIFY phone).
2. **Build-phase carousel (5 cards or fewer).** EXAMPLE: "One custom build, phase by phase." Card 1 "Lot and land" / Card 2 "Foundation" / Card 3 "Framing" / Card 4 "Systems" / Card 5 "Finishes and final walkthrough." Real Matchett photos only.
3. **Questions to ask any builder (plan: Nov 18 graphic).** EXAMPLE: "5 questions to ask a builder before you sign: Who is on site every day? How are change orders priced? What is in the allowances? Who do I call? What is the warranty in writing?" (Do not state our own answers until Chris gives them.)
4. **Project photo with a one-line story.** EXAMPLE: "Treetops to trusses." Photo of a real Matchett build; caption names the town only if the client allows.
5. **Testimonial card.** Name and town at the top, signed release first.
6. **Local callout.** EXAMPLE: "Own land in Cherokee, Fannin, Gilmer or Pickens County?" then "Let's talk about what it takes to build on it." Service counties are on the site.

## Reels: the hook system

- Audio hook = Chris's first spoken line. Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second, so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is being spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "The hardest decision happens before anyone pours a footing." Visual: "BEFORE THE FOOTING". Audio: "Here is what I show every client before we frame." Visual: "WHAT YOU SEE FIRST". Audio: "Land costs are the number most people forget." Visual: "THE HIDDEN LINE ITEM".
- No talking head: first frame is the build (framing, a lot, a finished room), not a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new card every 2 to 3 seconds, no logo intro.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge; hook upper-middle centered; burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = hook frame. Large type for the 60-plus audience.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5 per the weekly pack.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio": "Call (VERIFY number) or visit matchettcustomhomes.com."
- First line carries the keyword (custom home builder North Georgia, Canton, Ellijay).
- Holidays are thank-you only. No prices, timelines or warranties unless the number is in Chris's own words.
