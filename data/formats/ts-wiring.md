# TS Wiring: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (none found). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us, not a competitor's. No own-account data, so nothing is PROVEN. Social media is deferred per the Jul 9 discovery meeting.

Rules: real job-site photos, no stock; testimonials need a signed release; no invented projects, prices or awards; license and years from the owners in their words.

## Graphic and static formats
1. **Scope card.** EXAMPLE: "Commercial wiring. Office. Retail. Warehouse. Manufacturing." / "Woodstock, Georgia."
2. **Job-site photo with one fact.** EXAMPLE: "Panel to plug: one warehouse buildout." Real job only.
3. **Credential card.** EXAMPLE: "Georgia unrestricted contractor license." (VERIFY the exact license wording.)
4. **Questions to ask a commercial electrician.** EXAMPLE carousel (5 cards or fewer): "Are you licensed for this scope?" / "Who is the project lead?" / "What is in the schedule?" / "How are changes priced?" / "Who do I call?"
5. **Testimonial card.** Name and company at the top, signed release first.
6. **Business card reveal.** EXAMPLE: "New card. Scan to see the work." (QR code decision, Jul 9.)

## Reels: the hook system
- Audio hook = first spoken line from the talking head (owner). Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "Here is what happens before the lights come on in a new warehouse." Visual: "BEFORE LIGHTS ON". Audio: "Twenty years of commercial wiring, and this is the part nobody sees." Visual: "ABOVE THE CEILING".
- No talking head: first frame is the panel, the conduit run or the job site, never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third. Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)
- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio": "tswiring.com and (VERIFY phone)." The website is a "Launching Soon" page until the rebuild.
- First line carries the keyword (commercial electrician Woodstock GA).
