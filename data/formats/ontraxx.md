# OnTraxx: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (none found). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us, not a competitor's. No own-account data, so nothing is PROVEN.

Rules: real footage only. Only 7 ft of track exists, so shoot segments and use forced perspective per the shot list; never claim a track length. No claim about speed, capacity, safety or damage prevention until the client confirms with data.

## Graphic and static formats
1. **Product close-up card.** EXAMPLE: "Wheel meets rail." Close-up photo of the wheel entering the guide track.
2. **One-person card.** EXAMPLE: "One person. One plane." Only if the client confirms.
3. **Tight hangar before and after.** EXAMPLE: "Before: pushed by hand. After: guided." Real install only.
4. **Who it is for.** EXAMPLE carousel (5 cards or fewer): "Private owners" / "Flight schools" / "FBOs" / "Hangar operators" / "Ask us."
5. **Install day card.** EXAMPLE: "The track goes down in a day." (VERIFY the time.)
6. **Brand card.** EXAMPLE: "OnTraxx hangar guidance." (patriotic logo variant on file.)

## Reels: the hook system
- Audio hook = first spoken line from the talking head (client or a pilot, with release). Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "Most hangar damage happens when you move the plane, not when you fly it." Visual: "MOVE IT RIGHT" (only if the client can support the claim). Audio: "Watch one person put this plane away." Visual: "ONE PERSON".
- No talking head: first frame is the wheel rolling toward the rail (shot list segments), never a logo or the whole 7 ft track. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new segment every 2 to 3 seconds; cutaway to a testimonial line or a slow-motion wheel insert.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third. Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)
- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio" (not recorded; VERIFY).
- First line carries the keyword (hangar guidance system).
