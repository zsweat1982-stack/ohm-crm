## Reels: the hook system
- Audio hook = first spoken line from the talking head (Jonathan Carver or a released customer). Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "Here is what happens before the drywall goes up." Visual: "BEFORE THE WALL CLOSES". Audio: "Most people do not know what a rough-in is." Visual: "WHAT IS A ROUGH-IN?"
- No talking head: first frame is the pipe, the fitting or the water heater, never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third. Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)
- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is a phone plus the website, never "link in bio": "Call 706-669-9285." The website address is unconfirmed (VERIFY) so use the phone until it is.
- First line carries the keyword (plumber Ellijay GA, new construction plumbing North Georgia).
