Evidence labels: TESTED = our own ad data, thin sample (brain). LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (none found). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us in this style, not a competitor's. Nothing is PROVEN (zero booked jobs from ad leads as of Nov 8).

## Graphic and static formats

Our own data (TESTED, thin): the dark-block style (photo on top, navy block, gold Khand headline, gold pill) got about 3 leads and is the style Zac wants. Rules: one specific scene, one checkable detail, no slogans, no full-bleed layouts, no extra elements in quote graphics, white caps not green, ads must not look identical.
1. **Ladder scene.** EXAMPLE: photo of a ladder against a roofline, headline "18 feet up. Every year." / body "We hang them, fix them all season and take them down." CTA: "Free quote at americasbestlights.com or call (719) 600-7852." (the 18 feet is from the research language; use it only if it matches the shown house.)
2. **Real quote card (no price floor).** EXAMPLE: "A real quote, in writing, before you book." / "We reply within 12 hours." (landing page promise; confirm Briley can keep it).
3. **Lit before Thanksgiving.** EXAMPLE: "Book by Nov 15. Lights up before Thanksgiving." Real deadline; pause Nov 16.
4. **Takedown card.** EXAMPLE: "We hang them. We fix them. We take them down." (never "storage").
5. **Visualizer card.** EXAMPLE: "See your house in lights first." screenshot of the free visualizer.
6. **Permanent system (ski towns and higher-income ZIPs).** EXAMPLE: "Permanent lights: yours year-round." Free holiday install only as "with a permanent system of $5,000 or more, up to a $1,000 value."
OBSERVED competitor patterns (Nov 8 and 9): "never hang lights again" is crowded; price-led permanent ads and discounts dominate; Springs installers are generic. Do not copy "invisible by day" or "800 customers trust" (unverified for ABLS).

## Reels: the hook system

- Audio hook = the first spoken line from the talking head (Briley, installers). Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken. Briley is open to filming (drone, installs, trailer prep).
- Examples. Audio: "Here is what a real holiday lighting quote looks like." Visual: "NO PRICE GAMES". Audio: "You do not need to climb that ladder again." Visual: "NO MORE LADDER". Audio (install day): "Two hours, 150 feet, done." Visual: "2 HOURS" (landing page claim, VERIFY).
- No talking head: first frame is the house, the ladder or the lit roofline (lights lit only in real footage), never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA: "Free quote at americasbestlights.com or call (719) 600-7852."
- First line carries the keyword (Christmas light installation Colorado Springs).
- Offer captions end with their full conditions. Thanksgiving: thank-you only.
