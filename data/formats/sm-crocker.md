Evidence labels: LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (none found). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. Rules: no invented projects, prices, awards or years. Veteran-owned is on the site; Veterans Day is thank-you only. Releases for anyone on camera.

## Graphic and static formats
1. **Lead with what you build, and where.** EXAMPLE: "Custom homes in Middle Tennessee." / "Veteran-owned. Over 20 years." (site claim, VERIFY) / "Request a free estimate at smcrockerconstruction.com."
2. **Service split card.** EXAMPLE: "Custom homes. Spec houses. Post frame. Roofing. Concrete." Each its own post.
3. **Project photo with one fact.** EXAMPLE: "Post frame shell, framed in [days]." Only with a confirmed real number.
4. **Process carousel (5 cards or fewer).** EXAMPLE: "Slab" / "Frame" / "Roof" / "Finish" / "Walkthrough."
5. **Owner on camera card.** EXAMPLE: "Meet the builder." (release).
6. **Veterans Day thank-you.** No offer.

## Reels: the hook system
- Audio hook = first spoken line from the owner or builder. Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "Twenty years of building in Middle Tennessee, and this is the part nobody sees." Visual: "INSIDE THE WALL". Audio: "Here is a post frame building, start to finish." Visual: "FRAME TO ROOF".
- No talking head: first frame is the build site or the finished room, never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third. Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)
- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus a phone, never "link in bio": "Request a free estimate at smcrockerconstruction.com." No phone appears on the home page (VERIFY).
- First line carries the keyword (custom home builder Middle Tennessee).
