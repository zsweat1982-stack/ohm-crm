# URC: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: PROVEN = our own account data (small samples, and a group-check caveat). LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days (none found). OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us, not a competitor's.

Plan look: the roof and the crew. Close shingle and flashing detail, tear-off day, commercial jobs. Plain and proud. Hard rules (plan): Georgia law, so describe the ROOF, never the insurance claim. Never "we handle your claim," "we work with your insurance," "you may be entitled," "$0 out of pocket," "we cover your deductible," "free roof." Commercial work is the safe harbor. Archive drone clips need Part 107 provenance. Month of Giving (PTSD Foundation of America) only if URC confirms it for 2026 in writing. No offer or CTA on Thanksgiving. Rep names and earnings claims need owner OK before reuse. A release on file for everyone on camera.

## Graphic and static formats

Our own data (PROVEN, small sample, FB Jul 1 to Sep 30): rep milestone and portrait graphics got 3x to 10x the reach of job and promo posts ($2 million milestone 13,285; rep spotlights 7,365, 6,395, 5,196; halfway through the year 2,658). Confirm the owner approved each rep claim before reuse.
1. **Rep or crew spotlight card.** EXAMPLE: "Meet [Name], project manager." / "Explains every step before the first shingle comes off." Name and claim only with the person's and Carrie's OK. (Praise in reviews names people.)
2. **Parts and counts card.** EXAMPLE: "What is in a roof quote?" / "Shingles. Flashing. Vents. Drip edge. Decking." (Reddit homeowners judge by counts, PROXY.)
3. **Tear-off day photo.** EXAMPLE headline: "Tear-off day. Down to the decking." Real URC job photo.
4. **Same-roof before and after.** EXAMPLE: "Same roof. Same angle." Real URC jobs only.
5. **Commercial TPO card.** EXAMPLE: "Flat roof on a commercial building? Ask about TPO seams." Safe-harbor content for property managers. VERIFY technical claims with the crew.
6. **Thank-you graphic (Veterans Day, Thanksgiving).** No offer, no CTA.
OBSERVED competitor patterns we do NOT copy: price anchors "starting at $7,995" and "$14,999 + FREE gutters" (price and "free" claims need URC's own confirmed numbers), and storm-damage or claim headlines.

## Reels: the hook system

- Audio hook = first spoken line from the talking head (crew lead or rep with a release). Visual hook = different text on screen in the first second so a sound-off viewer stays. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "Here is what is actually in a roof quote." Visual: "COUNT THE VENTS". Audio: "This is what the roof looks like before the new one goes on." Visual: "TEAR-OFF DAY". Audio (commercial): "If you manage a flat roof, ask about the seams." Visual: "TPO SEAMS".
- No talking head: first frame is the shingle, the flashing or the crew on the roof, never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = hook frame.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio": "Call (770) 685-0225 or visit urcroof.com." Georgia posts use the Buford number only.
- First line carries the keyword (roof replacement Buford GA, commercial roofing Atlanta).
- No insurance words. Holidays thank-you only.
