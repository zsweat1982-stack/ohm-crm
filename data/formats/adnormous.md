# Adnormous: formats, copy and text on screen

Evidence labels: PROVEN = our own account data (small samples). LONG-RUNNER = competitor ad running 365+ days. OBSERVED = seen live, no performance data. PROXY = indirect. RULE = OHM standard. EXAMPLE = copy written by us, not a competitor's.

Scope: Adnormous is full large-format printing (walls, windows, storefronts, signage, trade show, banners, fleet and vehicle wraps), not only wraps. Plan look: transformation first, blank to finished, material and process close-ups. Rules: real jobs only; before and after from the same angle on the same vehicle or storefront; no customer logo or name without written permission; no price, turnaround or certification claim unless Mark says it. Veterans Day is thank-you only. Black Friday offer only if Mark confirms in writing.

## Graphic and static formats

Holiday posts got 15 to 19 views.
1. **Size-limit card.** EXAMPLE: "Most printers hit a size limit. We don't." then "Walls, windows, buildings, buses. Designed, printed and installed by one crew." CTA: "Call 770.333.2790 or visit adnormous.com."
2. **Blank to finished, same angle (storefront or wall, not only vehicles).** EXAMPLE: "This wall starts blank." Two photos from one spot. Real job only.
3. **Scale proof.** EXAMPLE: "Three city blocks. One takeover." Only for the real job with the client's OK.
4. **Speed card.** EXAMPLE: "A few weeks, or days?" Only with a turnaround Mark confirms (site says "1 wk typical on big jobs," VERIFY).
5. **What we print (scope carousel, 5 cards).** EXAMPLE: "Walls." "Windows." "Storefronts." "Trade show floors." "Fleets and buses."
6. **Process close-up.** EXAMPLE: "Edges trimmed. Seams matched. Done." A close-up of the install.
Long-runner pattern (OBSERVED): a plain trust card that states the proof ("100% 5 Star Google Reviews / In Business Since 2008," Signature Graphics, about 423 days). Adnormous has 14 Google reviews, so use "Est. 2006" (on the site, VERIFY) rather than a review claim.

## Reels: the hook system

- Audio hook = first spoken line from the talking head (Mark or the crew lead). Visual hook = different on-screen text in the first second, for sound-off viewers. Do not print what is spoken.
- Examples. Audio: "Most printers stop at a size limit. Here is what that looks like." Visual: "NO SIZE LIMIT". Audio: "We measure the vehicle before we print anything." Visual: "MEASURE FIRST" (only if Mark confirms the process). Audio (install): "This storefront was blank this morning." Visual: "BLANK TO DONE".
- No talking head: the first frame is the blank surface or the huge print, never a logo. Text cards 8 words or fewer, new shot every 2 to 3 seconds, same-angle before and after.
- Placement (RULE, Meta Reels safe zones, verify): text out of the top 14% and bottom 35%, away from the right edge, hook upper-middle centered, burned-in captions in the middle third.
- Cover frame = hook frame. The HP install video (Oct 28 shoot, Drive folder "HP Install 10-28-26") is the lead proof piece.

## Captions (RULE)

- Order: hook, body, one call to action. No hashtags on Instagram; Facebook up to 5.
- CTA is the website plus phone, never "link in bio": "Get a quote at adnormous.com or call 770.333.2790."
- First line carries the keyword (large format printing Atlanta, wall graphics, fleet wraps Atlanta).
- Holidays: thank-you only. No invented offers.
