# Voice of customer to copy (how every ad and graphic line gets written)

Zac's rule (Nov 8, 2026): the copy comes from what real people say. Reddit threads and comments, competitors' 1 to 3 star reviews, competitors' 5 star reviews, and what people type into Google. We read between the lines: the complaint on the surface is not the real problem. Everything here is research only. We never quote a competitor or a Reddit user in a public ad. We use the insight under it and tie it to something about the client that is true and checkable.

## The 6 steps
1. **Collect verbatim.** Pull quotes from the research pack (Reddit, Google Maps reviews, search questions). Keep the exact words and the source.
2. **Sort by what it is:** a fear (something they want to avoid), a desire (something they want), or an objection (why they would not buy).
3. **Read between the lines.** Ask: what is the person really afraid of or asking for? "It went over budget" is really "I lost control and nobody told me." "He never called back" is really "I paid and now I am alone."
4. **Match a stage.** Fears and objections usually fit Stage 2 (Pain). Desires fit Stage 3 (Promise). Praise phrases become Stage 4 (Proof) angles. Searches fit Stage 1.
5. **Write the line** so it is one person, one moment, and checkable. Then add the proof the client can back (a number, a process, a date). If the client cannot back it, mark VERIFY or do not use it.
6. **Log the source** (quote id, stage, angle, design) next to the ad so we know which insight produced which result.

## Rules
- A competitor's name or words never appear in our ads. We use the pattern and write our own line.
- If the verbatim needs a real person's words in our ad (a testimonial), we need their written release.
- Every row needs a "what the client must prove." If it is blank or VERIFY, the line does not ship.
- Keep a living table like this inside each client brain (section 6). New month, new rows.
