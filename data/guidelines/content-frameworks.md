# Content frameworks and static-ad style (applies to every client brain)

Added Nov 8, 2026 from three reference posts Zac saved (adswithsimon "first-line cheat sheet", fusedmedia "one lake, five levels", peter.visuals "power words"). These are other people's frameworks used as checklists, not copy to reuse. Evidence label for all of them: PROXY (popular and sensible, not proven for our clients). Only our own tests make something PROVEN.

## 1. First-line cheat sheet (adswithsimon, Nov 6 post, 150 likes)
Four traps in a first line, a question to ask, and the fix:
| Trap | Sounds like | Ask yourself | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crowd talk | "Tired of low energy?" | Is it one person in one moment? | Pick the person |
| Air claims | "Game-changing" | Can they check it? | Say something they can check |
| Labels | "Premium cookware" | Is it about them? | Talk to them, not about the product |
| Sorting line | "The cure for summer boredom" | One parent, one moment, about them | Keep it |

**Where it fits:** it is the same rule as our copy rules 4, 10 and 11, and the ads that work ("He said this weekend. Three weekends ago."). Use it as the final check on every hook before an editor, designer or ad goes out: one named person, one moment, one checkable detail. Reject "premium," "game-changing," "luxury," "dream home," "stress-free" with no proof.

## 2. Five awareness levels (fusedmedia, 147 likes)
One audience, five levels. Match the content to the level, never mix levels in one asset:
1. **Potential (don't know yet):** problem-curiosity or pattern-interrupt hooks. Organic reels and carousels. No offer.
2. **Pain (feel the pain):** name the exact moment. Customer language from the research pack.
3. **Promise (know what they want):** the outcome and what is included. Price or range shown when the client allows.
4. **Proof (know your service):** reviews, before and after, real job photos, process.
5. **Offer (ready to buy):** the offer with real terms, the deadline if real, one action.
This maps to the four stages already in the conversion-copywriter agent (never heard, knows the problem, comparing, ready). Every test plan must say which level each asset targets. Organic goes first for levels 1 to 3. Paid starts at levels 3 to 5 and retargeting.

## 3. Power words for hooks (peter.visuals, 3,427 likes)
Hidden, secret, costly, overlooked, unspoken, unknown, unexpected, surprising, shocking, controversial, unpopular, forbidden, banned, rare, weird, strange, brutal, painful, dangerous, risky, critical, crucial, essential, biggest, worst, deadliest, powerful, proven, simple, effortless, instant, immediately, quickly, finally, actually, suddenly, never, always, avoid, stop, warning, mistake, truth, lie, myth, trap, shortcut, insider, untold.
**Rules for using them (ours, not the post's):**
- A power word only goes in if the sentence under it is true and checkable. "The costly mistake in your quote" needs a real mistake and a real number.
- Do not use shock, forbidden, banned, secret or deadly words for regulated or sensitive clients (health care, nonprofits, schools, and any durability or performance claim). The client's Brain overrides this list.
- Strongest fits: costly, mistake, myth, truth, hidden (hidden costs), never, stop, avoid, what nobody tells you.
- A hook that stacks three power words reads as bait. One word, one fact.

## 4. Static ads, not posters (Zac's standard from Nov 8)
Our graphics should look like static ads that run on Meta: a real photo, one line, one detail, one button. Not an infographic poster.
- **Real photos only.** Photos from the client's own jobs, shop or site. Never AI images, never renders, never stock passed off as the client's work. If a photo is not confirmed as theirs, mark VERIFY.
- **Layout:** photo on top (about 60%), solid block under it, headline in two short lines, one sentence of detail, one button, domain and phone in the footer. Size 1080 x 1350 (4:5). Safe margins for Feed.
- **One idea per graphic.** One person or moment in the headline, one checkable detail in the sentence.
- **No extras:** no icons, borders, stickers, extra badges, gradients or decorative lines. Brand colors for the headline and button only, per the client brain.
- **Script:** `~/Downloads/Creative-System/static_ad_builder.py` builds this layout from a photo and a palette. A demo is in `~/Downloads/Creative-System/static-ads/`. Fonts are Khand Bold and Open Sans until each client's brand fonts are confirmed.
- **Every build records:** the photo file, who confirmed it is the client's, every claim in the text with its source, and the level (1 to 5) it targets.
