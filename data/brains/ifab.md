## 6. Customer language and fears (Nov 8 research)
Sources: Reddit 7 searches (443 rows, I read about 70 closely, mostly national, mostly homeowner pool and landscape and general concrete), Google (7 searches with AI Overview), Google Maps reviews of 6 Atlanta-area competitors (166 reviews, 143 with text). Competitor words are research only.
- **Scope in writing is the fear.** Reddit: "A vague concrete quote can turn into two people thinking they agreed to different jobs" and "base/depth, drainage plan, slope, joints, access/cleanup, and what happens if they uncover something weird." People want it spelled out before anyone starts.
- **Price transparency earns trust.** A pondless waterfall builder who shared his price got "Thank you for sharing (and the transparency on pricing)!"
- **Mock-ups reduce risk.** Concrete professionals say panel mock-ups are "pretty typical" before the full pour. A mock-up is a trust signal to show.
- **Looks natural or looks fake.** Waterfall threads: plants and surroundings "make or break" it; a big waterfall on a flat yard is "hard to keep looking natural." The fear is a result that looks cheap.
- **Competitor reviews (166, five-star heavy):** praise counts across 143 written reviews, keyword match, approximate: quality and craft 65, on time and deadlines 37, communication 36, professional and trust 35, custom and creative 32, price and budget 17, problem solving 10.
- **What gets praised, verbatim, research only:** "communicated with us at every step, often sending photos to explain what was happening" (Cherrylion Studios); "always deliver on time and have such great creative solutions" (Cherrylion); "kept the project on schedule and within budget" (Cherrylion).
- **What breaks trust (5 low-star reviews with text):** silence and slow replies ("They took a month to get back to me on having signs made," FASTSIGNS; "It took four weeks for our sign to be installed"; "worst customer service"), and damage or quality issues after the job ("the gravel and dust they used killed a large portion of our lawn," Outdoor Makeover).
- **Searches (Google):** people ask what a scenic fabricator or museum exhibit fabricator does, who builds themed rockwork in Georgia, and Atlanta trade show exhibit builders. Google lists iFab at 1491 Trae Ln under "themed environments, 3D sculptures, museum displays, trade show booths and large-scale props" for "scenic fabrication atlanta."
- **Not found:** B2B buyer language (LinkedIn blocked), real search volumes, iFab's own reviews (only 2), Facebook groups, Nextdoor.
- **Words to use:** built, brief, mock-up, install, deadline, in writing, itemized. **Avoid:** world-class, premier, "dream," unproven "on time every time."
