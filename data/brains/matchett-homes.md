## 6. Customer language and fears (research, Nov 8 2026)
Sources: Reddit 66 posts and 466 comments from 8 searches (1 incomplete, 3 not reached; I read about 60 of the 527 rows closely, so counts are not theme percentages). Google results for 7 searches, with AI Overview text. Mostly national Reddit, little North Georgia. Labels: OBSERVED unless marked.
- **Price is a range nobody trusts.** Reddit cites $200 to $450 per square foot depending on market and finish (examples: "$300+ west metro Minneapolis," "$400 in BC," "$250 for the cheapest custom builder rural SE"). Google's AI Overview puts Georgia mid-range custom at $270 to $350 per square foot and luxury at $350 to $500+. Quote gaps are driven by allowances and finishes: "Budgeted for $350 PSF and already over $400 less than halfway through."
- **Budget creep and change orders** are the main fear. Builders and homeowners both say change orders decide whether a project works or fails.
- **Land and site costs are the hidden number:** "Land. Land. And Land."
- **Trust in the builder and the money:** a homeowner on a cost-plus build worried the GC was in over his head and asked about lien waivers; replies say to demand notarized unconditional waivers before paying.
- **Choosing a builder:** "talk to 3 builders, ask square foot price, how they manage the project, the biggest hiccup. Ask for a design contract so you keep the plans." "Someone you want to be in a relationship with for the next 2 years."
- **Quote shopping:** "The person quoting you 500 sf does not want to build the kind of home you are describing." Big-name builders charge more; smaller regional builders are more flexible.
- **Timeline:** Google says 10 to 24 months from concept to move-in, 3 to 12 months of that before construction (design and permits). Scheduling delays and subcontractor coordination are the common complaints.
- **Red flags Google lists:** deposits over 10%, vague contracts, builders who resist questions.
- **What to ask a builder (Google):** change orders and budget adjustments, communication cadence, who is on site daily, references from someone under construction, someone just moved in, and someone 2 to 3 years in, fixed price vs cost-plus open book, written warranty.
- **Not found:** North Georgia specific Reddit threads, Facebook groups (Jasper, Ellijay), Nextdoor, Houzz. Competitor review text was pulled for 5 Canton builders (see section 7); Ellijay builders and Matchett's own reviews are not yet pulled.
- **Words to use:** budget, allowances, change orders, timeline, "what's included," land, draws. **Avoid:** luxury, dream home hype, "stress-free" with no proof.
