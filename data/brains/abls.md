## 6. Customer language (from the Nov 8 research pack, OBSERVED unless marked)
Sources: 129 Reddit threads, 1,248 comments, 1,522 reviews of 66 Colorado installers, Google People Also Ask and related searches. Full text: `Research/ABLS/2026-11-08/`.
- **The ladder is the pain:** fear of falling, bad back, age, "18 feet up every year." Reddit: weather 22%, ladder and safety 15%, price shock 14%, wants it professional 12% (keyword match, approximate).
- **Price confusion is the biggest search behavior:** "how much does it cost to pay someone to hang Christmas lights," "what's fair." Reddit replies range from $300 to over $3,000.
- **Winners are praised for easy, not cheap:** professional crew 39%, looks great 33%, on time 31%, hassle saved 30%, communication 21%.
- **Complaints come after the sale:** of 42 low-star reviews, price or fee surprises 38%, slow or no response 31%, spam texts 26%, app or light failures 24%, no-shows 10%.
- **Own search terms (Oct 16 to 31):** Search converted on "christmas light installation." Permanent cost questions appear ("how much are permanent lights on house"). Performance Max (now paused) wasted most volume on brand and DIY terms.
- **Gaps in the data:** no verified search volume, few Springs-specific Reddit threads, Facebook groups, Nextdoor and Yelp not pulled.
- **Words to use:** takedown, ladder, price up front, real quote, lit before Thanksgiving. **Words to avoid:** storage, cheap, deal, from $699 (Springs), 30 spots.
