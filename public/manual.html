Users Manual

How every part of the Open Heart Media CRM works, what to click, and who it is for. Read the page for your job. It takes five minutes.

Updated November 2026

Start here

  1. Sign in at app.openheartmediaco.com with your own work email and the temporary password Zac sent you privately.
  2. Pick your own password the first time. Use at least 10 characters. Nobody else ever needs it, including Zac.
  3. Look at Home. It is built around you: your work, what is late, your calendar, and your clients.
  4. Forgot it? Ask Zac or Griffin to reset it. You will get a new temporary one and pick your own again.

Basecamp links open Basecamp, which uses your own Basecamp login, so you see what you already have access to there. The CRM never shows Basecamp content to people who could not already see it.

Who sees what: everyone sees Home, Work, Mail, Content, Clients, Onboarding, How We Work and Conversions. The sales pipeline (Outreach) is limited to Lisa, Michelle, Brad, Griffin and Zac, and the server enforces it, so it is not just hidden.

Home

Your morning screen. Open it first.

  • Top cards: your open work, what is late across all clients, unread client mail, active clients, and (for sales) waiting replies. Click a card to jump there.
  • Needs attention: the short list of things that are late or waiting on you.
  • Agenda: today and the next days from your calendar.
  • Your clients: one tile per client with open and late counts. Click one to open its page.
  • Refresh pulls new data from Basecamp. If it says Basecamp is not connected, click Connect Basecamp once.

Work

Every open to-do across every client, in one list.

  • Filter to My work to see only what is assigned to you.
  • Late items are marked. Clear those first.
  • Click an item to open it in Basecamp and finish it there. Finishing it in Basecamp is what clears it here.
  • New client emails and tasks are routed to the person for that kind of work (see Who handles what on the Team page).

Mail

  1. Click Connect your Google account once. This links your own work mailbox, not anyone else's.
  2. Client email shows here so replies are not missed. Client replies are due the next business day.
  3. Reply from Gmail as normal. The CRM only reads and organizes.

Content: how a post gets made, start to finish

This is the flow every client follows. The Content page shows where each week stands.

  1. Plan. The monthly content calendar sets what posts on which day for each client. The film calendar shows the next shoots.
  2. Shot list. Before a shoot, the client's Weekly pack tab holds the shot list and timeline. Confirm job addresses with the client first.
  3. Shoot and upload. Upload to the client's Drive folder. Name every file CLIENT_TYPE_DATE_Subject_LABEL_001. Wrong names are the number one reason things get lost.
  4. Due Friday at 5:00 PM. Content for the next week is due every Friday. The Content page shows what was uploaded and what is missing.
  5. Edit and design. Editors and designers work from the uploads. Long pauses, filler words and messy talking-head edits go back to the editor.
  6. Caption. Open the client's Copy tab. Every caption has a hook, a body and a CTA, follows the client's own guidelines and the 16 copy rules, uses American spelling, and has no em dashes. Talking-head and testimonial posts need no topic, just the footage.
  7. Approve. Everything goes to To be approved by Zac in Basecamp. Four clients also need client approval: URC, Nitro Pro, BKVF and CISGA. Their folders are copied to the client approval folder and the right approvers are tagged. Griffin writes URC captions first, then the client approves.
  8. Post. Approved posts are scheduled in Sprout at that profile's best times, with the client's real call to action. Nothing posts before approval.

Rule of thumb: if you are unsure whether something is approved, it is not. Ask before posting.

Clients

One page per client. Open it from Clients or from your Home tiles.

  • Overview: open and late to-dos, the linked Basecamp project, Drive folder and key links. If the Basecamp project is not linked, pick it from the list and click Link.
  • Content: what is planned and posted for this client.
  • Weekly pack: the week's shot list and deliverables.
  • Copy: captions, hooks and the client's research bank of real customer language.
  • Brain: who the client is, offers, audience, and the facts that must never be wrong.
  • Formats and Look: the post formats they use and their brand look.
  • Guidelines: the client's own rules, including compliance. Read these before writing anything.

Onboarding

New clients fill in the onboarding form. Each submission lands here with its date, and a Basecamp project is created from it. If a submission shows no project, open it and link or create one. Then assign the first tasks.

Conversions (ads team)

Proof that ads create real leads, not just clicks.

  • Every real conversion is a form submit or a phone call of 60 seconds or more from an ad. Page opens and tap-to-call taps are secondary.
  • A small script on the client's site saves the ad click ID with each lead. When the lead becomes a booked job we send that back to Google (a file upload) and to Meta (a server event) so the ad platforms learn from real jobs.
  • V1 covers Visiting Nurse, Matchett, Sun & Shield and iFab. Calls come next.
  • The Conversions tab shows whether the conversion system is live and the setup steps for each client. Until it is live, the checklist there is the work to do.

Outreach (sales team only)

Lisa, Michelle, Brad, Griffin and Zac.

  • Pipeline columns: Queue, Drafted, Awaiting reply, Replied, Booked, Won. Click a lead for its card, the audit report and the email draft.
  • Sources: Inbound, Giveaway, No Website call list, and Manual adds.
  • Claim a lead by setting yourself as owner so two people do not email the same business.
  • Replies show on Home as soon as they arrive. Answer the same day.
  • Automatic sending has a pause button at the top. If anything looks wrong, pause first and ask second.
  • Never copy prospect data out of the CRM into chats or personal files.

How We Work

The agency field guide: our six services and how they fit together. Read it once when you start, and send new clients to the matching section.

Team (admins)

  • Add a person: enter name, email, job role. The CRM shows a temporary password once. Copy it and send it privately. Closing the page loses it.
  • Add the whole team: tick people and click Add selected to issue everyone's temporary password at once.
  • Reset password issues a new temporary one. Turn off blocks a login immediately, for example when someone leaves.
  • Pipeline: on/off controls who can see sales prospects and outreach.

Security, in plain words

  • The address is https only. Never use it on a public computer, and sign out on shared devices.
  • Use a password you use nowhere else. Do not share it. Ask for a reset instead.
  • Client logins, ad accounts and API keys never go in the CRM, chat or email. Ask Zac.
  • If you think someone else saw your password, tell Zac right away and it gets reset.
