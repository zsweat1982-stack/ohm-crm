One customer, start to finish

A local business, followed from the first video to a repeat buyer.

  1. Video. We film a customer testimonial and ten short opening hooks.
  2. Ads. The best clips run to new people nearby.
  3. Landing page. Someone taps and lands on one clear offer.
  4. Lead magnet. They trade their email for something useful.
  5. Retargeting. Next day, they see the testimonial.
  6. Reel it in. The client's team texts back within five minutes.
  7. Email. Not ready yet, so a welcome email and an FAQ video follow.
  8. Local search. They look the business up and find great reviews. They book.
  9. The log. The report shows which hook brought them. We shoot more like it.

Score the catch, not the bites

Clients keep paying for results they can see in their bank account. Report on customers, not attention.

Report these

  • Booked appointments and new customers
  • Cost per customer
  • Revenue from each service
  • How fast leads get a reply

Not these on their own

  • Likes and followers
  • Impressions and reach
  • Clicks
  • Number of posts made

This only works if the CRM shows which leads actually bought.

The first 90 days

Month one

Build

  • Agree on the ideal customer and the offer
  • Tracking, CRM and landing page
  • A lead follow-up plan with the client
  • The first video shoot
  • Welcome email and text
Month two

Launch

  • Ads go live with several different hooks
  • Retargeting turned on
  • Weekly: cut losers, fund winners
  • First report: leads, cost per lead, bookings
Month three

Grow

  • Shoot more of what won
  • Test the landing page
  • Add social or SEO
  • Report cost per customer and revenue
Before any ads go live, ask the client: who answers new leads, and how fast? If nobody calls back within minutes, fix that first.
